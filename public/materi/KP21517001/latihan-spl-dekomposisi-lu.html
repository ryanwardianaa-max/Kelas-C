Soal Latihan Nomor 2 · Metode Numerik

Penyelesaian SPL 4×4 dengan Metode Dekomposisi LU

Pembahasan lengkap, terverifikasi, dan langkah demi langkah (step-by-step) penyelesaian sistem persamaan lanjar matriks $4 \times 4$ menggunakan faktorisasi LU (Metode Doolittle).

Soal Nomor 2:
Diberikan sistem persamaan lanjar $A \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ dengan matriks $A$ dan vektor $\mathbf{b}$ sebagai berikut:
$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & -1 \\ 2 & 5 & 4 & 8 \\ 4 & 2 & 2 & 1 \\ 6 & 4 & -1 & -2 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b} = \begin{bmatrix} 10 \\ 8 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}$$
Tentukan solusinya dengan Metode Dekomposisi LU.

1. Gagasan dan Alur Kerja Dekomposisi LU (Doolittle)

Pada metode Dekomposisi LU (Doolittle):

  1. Matriks koefisien $A$ difaktorkan menjadi hasil kali dua matriks segitiga: $$A = L \cdot U$$ di mana $L$ adalah matriks segitiga bawah dengan elemen diagonal bernilai 1 ($l_{ii} = 1$), dan $U$ adalah matriks segitiga atas hasil eliminasi Gauss.
  2. Persamaan $A \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ diubah menjadi $L(U \mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{b}$. Misalkan $\mathbf{y} = U \mathbf{x}$.
  3. Langkah 1 (Substitusi Maju): Selesaikan $L \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{b}$ untuk memperoleh vektor antara $\mathbf{y}$.
  4. Langkah 2 (Substitusi Mundur): Selesaikan $U \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{y}$ untuk memperoleh solusi akhir $\mathbf{x}$.

2. Tahap 1: Faktorisasi $A = L \cdot U$ (Eliminasi Gauss Bertahap)

Langkah 1: Eliminasi Kolom Pertama (Pivot $a_{11} = 1$)

Baris 1 matriks $U$ adalah baris 1 matriks $A$:

$$U_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & -1 \end{bmatrix}$$

Hitung pengali eliminasi untuk menolkan elemen di bawah $a_{11}$:

  • Pengali baris 2: $m_{21} = \frac{a_{21}}{u_{11}} = \frac{2}{1} = 2 \implies l_{21} = 2$
    $$R_2 \gets R_2 - 2 R_1 = [2, 5, 4, 8] - 2[1, 2, 3, -1] = [0, 1, -2, 10]$$
  • Pengali baris 3: $m_{31} = \frac{a_{31}}{u_{11}} = \frac{4}{1} = 4 \implies l_{31} = 4$
    $$R_3 \gets R_3 - 4 R_1 = [4, 2, 2, 1] - 4[1, 2, 3, -1] = [0, -6, -10, 5]$$
  • Pengali baris 4: $m_{41} = \frac{a_{41}}{u_{11}} = \frac{6}{1} = 6 \implies l_{41} = 6$
    $$R_4 \gets R_4 - 6 R_1 = [6, 4, -1, -2] - 6[1, 2, 3, -1] = [0, -8, -19, 4]$$

Langkah 2: Eliminasi Kolom Kedua (Pivot $u_{22} = 1$)

Baris 2 matriks $U$ adalah baris 2 hasil eliminasi kolom 1:

$$U_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 & -2 & 10 \end{bmatrix}$$

Hitung pengali eliminasi untuk baris 3 dan 4:

  • Pengali baris 3: $m_{32} = \frac{-6}{1} = -6 \implies l_{32} = -6$
    $$R_3 \gets R_3 - (-6) R_2 = [0, -6, -10, 5] + 6[0, 1, -2, 10] = [0, 0, -22, 65]$$
  • Pengali baris 4: $m_{42} = \frac{-8}{1} = -8 \implies l_{42} = -8$
    $$R_4 \gets R_4 - (-8) R_2 = [0, -8, -19, 4] + 8[0, 1, -2, 10] = [0, 0, -35, 84]$$

Langkah 3: Eliminasi Kolom Ketiga (Pivot $u_{33} = -22$)

Baris 3 matriks $U$ adalah baris 3 hasil eliminasi kolom 2:

$$U_3 = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & -22 & 65 \end{bmatrix}$$

Hitung pengali eliminasi untuk baris 4:

  • Pengali baris 4: $m_{43} = \frac{-35}{-22} = \frac{35}{22} \approx 1{,}590909 \implies l_{43} = \frac{35}{22}$
    $$\begin{aligned} R_4 \gets R_4 - \frac{35}{22} R_3 &= [0, 0, -35, 84] - \frac{35}{22}[0, 0, -22, 65] \\ &= \left[0, 0, 0,\ 84 - \frac{2275}{22}\right] = \left[0, 0, 0,\ \frac{1848 - 2275}{22}\right] = \left[0, 0, 0,\ -\frac{427}{22}\right] \end{aligned}$$
    Di mana $-\frac{427}{22} \approx -19{,}409091$.

Hasil Matriks Faktorisasi $L$ dan $U$

$$L = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 4 & -6 & 1 & 0 \\ 6 & -8 & \frac{35}{22} & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad U = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -2 & 10 \\ 0 & 0 & -22 & 65 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -\frac{427}{22} \end{bmatrix}$$

3. Tahap 2: Substitusi Maju ($L \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{b}$)

Persamaan matriks segitiga bawah:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 4 & -6 & 1 & 0 \\ 6 & -8 & \frac{35}{22} & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} y_1 \\ y_2 \\ y_3 \\ y_4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 10 \\ 8 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}$$
  1. Baris 1:
    $$1 \cdot y_1 = 10 \implies y_1 = \mathbf{10}$$
  2. Baris 2:
    $$2(10) + 1 \cdot y_2 = 8 \implies 20 + y_2 = 8 \implies y_2 = 8 - 20 = \mathbf{-12}$$
  3. Baris 3:
    $$4(10) - 6(-12) + 1 \cdot y_3 = -2 \implies 40 + 72 + y_3 = -2 \implies 112 + y_3 = -2 \implies y_3 = \mathbf{-114}$$
  4. Baris 4:
    $$\begin{aligned} 6(10) - 8(-12) + \frac{35}{22}(-114) + y_4 &= 4 \\ 60 + 96 + \frac{35 \times (-57)}{11} + y_4 &= 4 \\ 156 - \frac{1995}{11} + y_4 &= 4 \\ \frac{1716 - 1995}{11} + y_4 &= 4 \implies -\frac{279}{11} + y_4 = \frac{44}{11} \\ y_4 &= \frac{44 + 279}{11} = \mathbf{\frac{323}{11}} \approx 29{,}363636 \end{aligned}$$

Vektor perantara: $\mathbf{y} = \begin{bmatrix} 10 & -12 & -114 & \frac{323}{11} \end{bmatrix}^T$.

4. Tahap 3: Substitusi Mundur ($U \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{y}$)

Persamaan matriks segitiga atas:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & -2 & 10 \\ 0 & 0 & -22 & 65 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -\frac{427}{22} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \\ x_4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 10 \\ -12 \\ -114 \\ \frac{323}{11} \end{bmatrix}$$
  1. Baris 4: Mencari $x_4$
    $$-\frac{427}{22} x_4 = \frac{323}{11} \implies x_4 = \frac{323}{11} \times \left(-\frac{22}{427}\right) = \frac{323 \times (-2)}{427} = \mathbf{-\frac{646}{427}} \approx -1{,}512881$$
  2. Baris 3: Mencari $x_3$
    $$\begin{aligned} -22 x_3 + 65 x_4 &= -114 \\ -22 x_3 + 65\left(-\frac{646}{427}\right) &= -114 \\ -22 x_3 - \frac{41990}{427} &= -\frac{48678}{427} \\ -22 x_3 &= -\frac{48678 - 41990}{427} = -\frac{6688}{427} \\ x_3 &= \frac{-6688}{427 \times (-22)} = \mathbf{\frac{304}{427}} \approx 0{,}711944 \end{aligned}$$
  3. Baris 2: Mencari $x_2$
    $$\begin{aligned} 1 x_2 - 2 x_3 + 10 x_4 &= -12 \\ x_2 &= -12 + 2\left(\frac{304}{427}\right) - 10\left(-\frac{646}{427}\right) \\ x_2 &= -\frac{5124}{427} + \frac{608}{427} + \frac{6460}{427} = \frac{-5124 + 7068}{427} = \mathbf{\frac{1944}{427}} \approx 4{,}552693 \end{aligned}$$
  4. Baris 1: Mencari $x_1$
    $$\begin{aligned} 1 x_1 + 2 x_2 + 3 x_3 - 1 x_4 &= 10 \\ x_1 &= 10 - 2 x_2 - 3 x_3 + x_4 \\ x_1 &= 10 - 2\left(\frac{1944}{427}\right) - 3\left(\frac{304}{427}\right) + \left(-\frac{646}{427}\right) \\ x_1 &= \frac{4270}{427} - \frac{3888 + 912 + 646}{427} = \frac{4270 - 5446}{427} = -\frac{1176}{427} \end{aligned}$$ Sederhanakan pembilang dan penyebut (faktor bersama 7): $$x_1 = -\frac{1176 \div 7}{427 \div 7} = \mathbf{-\frac{168}{61}} \approx -2{,}754098$$

5. Ringkasan Solusi dan Verifikasi Perkalian Matriks $A \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$

Solusi Akhir Sistem Persamaan Lanjar:
Variabel Bentuk Pecahan Eksak Hampiran Desimal (6 Angka)
$x_1$ $-\frac{168}{61}$ ($-\frac{1176}{427}$) $-2{,}754098$
$x_2$ $\frac{1944}{427}$ $+4{,}552693$
$x_3$ $\frac{304}{427}$ $+0{,}711944$
$x_4$ $-\frac{646}{427}$ $-1{,}512881$

Verifikasi Substitusi ke Persamaan Awal ($A \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$):

  • Baris 1: $1\left(-\frac{1176}{427}\right) + 2\left(\frac{1944}{427}\right) + 3\left(\frac{304}{427}\right) - 1\left(-\frac{646}{427}\right) = \frac{-1176 + 3888 + 912 + 646}{427} = \frac{4270}{427} = \mathbf{10}$ (Tepat!)
  • Baris 2: $2\left(-\frac{1176}{427}\right) + 5\left(\frac{1944}{427}\right) + 4\left(\frac{304}{427}\right) + 8\left(-\frac{646}{427}\right) = \frac{-2352 + 9720 + 1216 - 5168}{427} = \frac{3416}{427} = \mathbf{8}$ (Tepat!)
  • Baris 3: $4\left(-\frac{1176}{427}\right) + 2\left(\frac{1944}{427}\right) + 2\left(\frac{304}{427}\right) + 1\left(-\frac{646}{427}\right) = \frac{-4704 + 3888 + 608 - 646}{427} = \frac{-854}{427} = \mathbf{-2}$ (Tepat!)
  • Baris 4: $6\left(-\frac{1176}{427}\right) + 4\left(\frac{1944}{427}\right) - 1\left(\frac{304}{427}\right) - 2\left(-\frac{646}{427}\right) = \frac{-7056 + 7776 - 304 + 1292}{427} = \frac{1708}{427} = \mathbf{4}$ (Tepat!)

Seluruh ruas kiri terbukti menghasilkan kembali ruas kanan $\mathbf{b} = [10,\ 8,\ -2,\ 4]^T$ secara eksak 100%.
